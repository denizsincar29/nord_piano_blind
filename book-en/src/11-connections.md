# Connections

All connectors are on the **rear panel**. Listed from left to right when facing the rear of the instrument.

---

## Power

### POWER ON/OFF
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, far left. Rectangular grey button.

A rectangular gray button. Press to turn the instrument on or off.

### AC IN
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, next to POWER ON/OFF. IEC power socket.

The mains power connector. Connect the supplied power cord here.

> Always turn on the Nord Piano 6 **before** your amplifier or active speakers. When shutting down, turn off the amplifier **first**.

---

## Audio Connections

### HEADPHONES
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, far right. 6.35mm stereo TRS jack.

**1/4 inch stereo jack.** Audio is always output through the headphones, regardless of the Output Routing setting in the Sound Menu. Controlled by the **MASTER LEVEL** knob.

### LEFT OUT and RIGHT OUT
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, right side. Two 6.35mm TS (mono) jacks.

**Two 1/4 inch unbalanced mono jacks** (standard TS connectors). These are line-level outputs for connecting to an amplifier, mixer, or recording interface.

The Nord Piano 6 is a stereo instrument with separate left and right signal paths. For stereo use, connect both outputs. For mono use, connect LEFT OUT only (or set Output to Mono in the Sound Menu to have both outputs carry an identical mono signal).

> The Master Level control affects these outputs.

### MONITOR IN
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, far right, next to HEADPHONES. 3.5mm stereo mini-jack.

**1/8 inch (3.5mm) stereo jack.** Used for connecting external devices such as smartphones, tablets, or computers to the Nord Piano 6 for playback during practice or performance.

The Monitor In signal is routed to both the Headphones output and the Left/Right outputs simultaneously, making it audible through any connected speakers.

> The **Master Level** knob does **not** affect the Monitor In signal level.

---

## MIDI Connections

### MIDI IN
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, center. 5-pin DIN connector.

**5-pin DIN connector.** Receives MIDI data from external devices such as controller keyboards, sequencers, computers (via MIDI interface), or other instruments.

### MIDI OUT
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, center, left of MIDI IN. 5-pin DIN connector.

**5-pin DIN connector.** Sends MIDI data to external sound modules, computers, or other MIDI devices.

> MIDI over USB and the 5-pin MIDI connectors are always active simultaneously. Both can transmit and receive MIDI at the same time.

---

## USB Connection

**USB Type B connector** (square shape with cut upper corners — the type found on printers and audio interfaces).

The USB connection is used for:
- Transferring MIDI to and from a computer (DAW, sequencer, Nord Sound Manager)
- OS updates
- Connecting to **Nord Sound Manager** for sound library management
- Connecting to **Nord Sample Editor 4** for creating custom sample instruments

Download both applications and the latest OS from **nordkeyboards.com**.

---

## Pedal Connections

### TRIPLE PEDAL
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, right of USB. Round multi-pin connector. Nord Triple Pedal 2 only.

A round multi-pin connector. **Only compatible with the Nord Triple Pedal 2 (TP-2).** The Triple Pedal 2 provides three pedals: right (sustain), middle (configurable), and left (configurable). Configure the middle and left pedal functions in the **Pedal Menu → Triple Pedal Function**.

### SUSTAIN PEDAL
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, right side. 6.35mm TS jack.

**1/4 inch (6.35mm) TS jack.** Accepts all common sustain pedals, as well as the **Nord Triple Pedal 1 (TP-1)** (which connects here, not to the Triple Pedal jack). Pedal type and polarity are set in the **Pedal Menu → Sustain Pedal Type**.

### VOL/CTRL PEDAL
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, right side. 6.35mm TS jack.

**1/4 inch (6.35mm) TS jack.** For a continuous expression pedal. Used for controlling Piano and/or Sample Synth volume, and/or the amount of Mod 1 effect. Most common expression pedal models are supported; set the type in **Pedal Menu → Control Pedal → Type**.

### FOOT SWITCH
📍 **Location:** Rear panel, left of USB. 6.35mm TS jack.

**1/4 inch (6.35mm) TS jack.** For a momentary single- or dual-button footswitch (not a continuous pedal). Can be assigned to functions such as switching programs or controlling Sample Synth Vibrato. Configure in **Pedal Menu → Foot Switch**.

> The Nord SP-2 and other continuous expression pedals are **not** compatible with the Foot Switch jack.

---

## Nord Piano Monitor

The Nord Piano 6 can be fitted with brackets for **Nord Piano Monitor** speakers, which are specifically designed for accurate piano sound reproduction. For information on compatible models and how to attach them, see nordkeyboards.com.
