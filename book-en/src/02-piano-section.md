# PIANO Section

## Description from left to right

At the top left is the round **MASTER LEVEL** regulator. This is the main level regulator for adjusting the overall volume. In the extreme left position - minimum, when rotating to the right - increase.

Below it is the gray rectangular **VIBRATO** button. Below the button is a round indicator, to the right of which is the inscription: ENABLE. This is the vibrato button, which can be used to manually control the synthesizer vibrato samples. It can be used to apply vibrato in real time based on frequency and amount settings made in the Sample Synth section. The ENABLE parameter (Shift+Vibrato) can be used to enable or disable this function for each layer, allowing vibrato to be applied using the button to one layer but not the other, for example, in split mode.

## PIANO Section

To the right is the next section with buttons and regulators, its name is PIANO.

It includes a wide range of piano and keyboard sounds from the ever-growing Nord Piano library, with new sounds regularly available for free at nordkeyboards.com.

The section is highlighted only visually: light gray background of the header, a frame of the same color and a dark gray background inside the frame.

### Left half of the PIANO section

At the top left of this section are two vertical slider regulators for two levels. These are layer A and B faders, which consist of a physical slider and an LED graph that provides a visual indication of the current output level of the layer.

They are adjusted using gray knobs shaped like cubes, slightly tapering towards the top. To the right of both regulators are vertical indicator scales. There are 12 rectangular sections in each scale, the level is displayed by filling these scales with green backlighting.

Under each slider there is a gray button: under the left one - for level A (LAYER A), under the right one - for level B (LAYER B). Above the button is the inscription ON/OFF with a down arrow, below is an indicator: a small round orange light bulb with an inscription to the right LAYER A (for the second button: LAYER B).

There are two Piano layers: A and B. Turning on the second layer is done by pressing both layer buttons simultaneously. To turn off a layer, you need to hold down its button for a short period of time. To switch from one layer to another, press the button of the inactive layer. If both layers are active, the selected layer is displayed by a blinking indicator.

Pressing the layer button also allows you to focus the effects of this layer for editing, as evidenced by the FX FOCUS indicator in the EFFECTS section (it will be described below), and the layer indicator itself will light up red.

Below each of the buttons are two more small round KB ZONE green glow indicators: LO and UP. Press the KB ZONE button (Shift + LAYER A or B) to assign the focused layer to any of the active keyboard zones. Green LEDs indicate whether the layer is assigned to LO (lower), UP (upper), or both keyboard zones.

Below is the heading OCTAVE SHIFT with a left arrow and a right arrow on either side of the name.

Below are two black buttons. Use them to move the selected piano layer up or down by octaves (+/- 12 semitones). If it is assigned to the entire keyboard, it can be moved one octave up or down. When assigned to a smaller zone, the available transposition values will differ, but will always provide access to the full piano sound range.

Under the left button is the inscription SUST PED, to the left of the inscription is a round red glow indicator. This is the sustain setting (Shift + left OCTAVE SHIFT button), it activates any connected sustain pedal for the selected piano layer.

The right button is signed below VOL PED, to the left of the inscription is a round red glow indicator. This is the volume control (Shift + right OCTAVE SHIFT button), it activates the connected volume control/adjustment pedal for the selected Piano layer. The LED fader can still be used to set the maximum volume level of the selected layer.
