# EFFECTS Section

To the right is the EFFECTS section. It is divided into rectangular blocks that are outlined with light gray lines and have a dark gray background.

## General Description of Working with the EFFECTS Section

Each piano and sample synthesizer layer has its own independent effects chain, which allows you to use different effects and settings for each sound source. The only exception is the GLOBAL REVERB global reverb effect, which is used by all layers in the program. However, if the reverb mode is set to LAYER SEND mode, its Dry/Wet sound level can be adjusted individually for each layer - all other reverb settings remain common.

To turn on an effect, press the corresponding ON button. Equalizer, compression, and delay effects can be switched to global mode, which applies the effect to all piano and sample synthesizer layers. To do this, press Shift+On for this effect. When global mode is turned off, all layers will use the same settings for this effect until they are manually changed.

## FX ON/OFF and Layer Focus

The left upper block of the EFFECTS section.

A round red glow indicator with the inscription FX ON, and below it is a gray button outlined in red. You can press it to instantly turn all effects on or off at once.

Below is the inscription PIANO on a light gray background. Below it are two round indicators: the left one is called A, and the right one is B. Below them is a black button, below it is the inscription GROUP.

Below is the inscription SYNCH on a light gray background. Below it are two round indicators: the left one is called A, and the right one is B. Below them is a black button, below it is the inscription GROUP.

The PIANO and SYNCH buttons allow you to focus a specific layer for editing. The current FX layer focus is displayed by LEDs A and B. Using GROUP (combination of Shift + Piano or Synth buttons) you can group effects for piano and/or synthesizer section. In group mode, effects for layers A and B are linked, and all changes immediately affect both layers.

## MOD1 Block

The next block of the EFFECTS section is called MOD1, located to the right of the previous one, and in height it is half its size. The MOD1 block offers six different types of modulation effects. There is an effect selection button, RATE regulator - effect speed, AMOUNT regulator - effect intensity.

At the top left is a group of indicators. The indicators look like pairs of red glow triangles pointing left and right. There are three such pairs, they are located one below the other.

Effects available:
- **PAN2** (top left) - provides harder panning, which is more consistent with the traditional vibrato effect often used in electric pianos
- **PAN1** (middle left) - automatic panning modulation that smoothly moves the signal between the left and right sides of the stereo panorama
- **TREM** (bottom left) - provides smooth volume modulation that continuously changes the output volume level
- **SPIN** (top right) - simulates the sound of a rotating speaker
- **WAH** (middle right) - WAH effect gives the sound a characteristic quacking character
- **RM** (bottom right) - ring modulation is a type of modulation in which two signals multiplied by each other create a harmonic sound similar to the sound of a bell

Below is a black effect selection button, below it is a round red glow indicator with the inscription CTRLPED. CTRLPED (Shift + Mod1 selection button) regulates effect control using a pedal.

To the right is a round stepped regulator knob with the inscription RATE (effect speed). The regulator has 11 numbered positions. In the extreme left position - 0, in the vertical top position - 5, in the extreme right position - 10.

To the right is a round stepped regulator knob with the inscription AMOUNT (effect intensity). The regulator has 11 numbered positions. In the extreme left position - 0, in the vertical top position - 5, in the extreme right position - 10.

To the right is a vertical gray button to activate the effect. Above it is a round red glow indicator with the inscription ON.

## MOD2 Block

The next block of the EFFECTS section is called MOD2 and is located below the previous one. In height it is equal to the MOD 1 block and therefore in total with the MOD1 block in height it is equal to the first left block of the EFFECTS section. The MOD2 block offers a selection of modulation effects.

Effects available:
- **FLANG** (top left) - flanger creates a comb filter effect, giving the sound a whistling and resonant quality
- **PHAS2** (middle left) - phaser effect creates a characteristic sweep effect commonly used in electric piano sound playback
- **PHAS1** (bottom left) - another variant of the phaser effect
- **CHOR1** (top right) - creates the impression of sound expansion, and with more extreme settings - strong deterioration of its sound, which is achieved by mixing several modulated copies of the audio signal
- **CHOR2** (middle right) - based on a classic pedal effect that in standard stereo mode provides rich stereo expansion
- **VIBE** (bottom right) - based on a classic foot pedal effect and creates a sound that changes pitch and phasing

Below is a black effect selection button, below it is a round red glow indicator with the inscription MONO. By default, all effects in the Mod 2 module operate in stereo mode. Press MONO (Shift + Mod 2 selection button) to switch to monophonic processing.

## EQUALIZER Block

At the top right of the EFFECTS section is a block called EQUALIZER.

It has four round stepped regulator knobs and a button to activate the effect placed horizontally next to each other. A three-band equalizer is equipped with bass regulators, an adjustable middle band, and treble.

Regulators from left to right:
- **BASS** regulator has 7 positions: -15, -10, -5, 0, 5, 10, 15
- **GAIN** regulator has 7 positions: -15, -10, -5, 0, 5, 10, 15
- **FREQ** regulator has 9 positions: 200, 300, 500, 800, 1K, 2K, 4K, 6K, 8K
- **TREBLE** regulator has 7 positions: -15, -10, -5, 0, 5, 10, 15

The BASS frequency is set to 100 Hz, the TREBLE frequency is set to 4 kHz, and the adjustable middle band can be set in the range from 200 Hz to 8 kHz using the FREQ regulator. The gain/attenuation range is +/- 15 dB for all bands.

To the right of the regulators is a vertical gray button to activate the effect. Above it is a round red glow indicator with the inscription ON, and below it is the same indicator with the inscription GLOBAL. The equalizer can be switched to GLOBAL mode (Shift+On), which applies the same settings to all piano and sample synthesizer layers.

## DELAY Block

Below the EQUALIZER block is a DELAY block of the same size. This block creates echo/repeat effects.

On the left in this block under the inscription TEMPO on a light gray background is a round indicator with the inscription TAP/SET and a down arrow. Below the indicator is a black button. Below, not on a gray background, is the inscription PING PONG and below it are two round indicators: 1 and 2.

The delay time is set by pressing the TEMPO button or by pressing it and setting the tempo using the regulator in the Program section. When PING PONG mode is enabled (Shift + Tempo), delay repeats alternate between left and right channels. Three settings are available (1, 2, and 3 with both LEDs on).

To the right is a round stepped FEEDBACK regulator knob. It regulates the number of delay repeats. At the lowest setting, only the initial repeat is heard, while at the highest, a very long feedback signal is created. The regulator has 11 positions from 0 to 10.

To the right under the heading FB FILTER are two round red glow indicators. Below is a black button. Here tools for shaping delayed sound are activated: each subsequent delay repeat is passed through a filter, resulting in the sound becoming increasingly filtered.
- **HP** (top) - provides for the use of a high-pass filter that removes low-frequency content
- **LP** (bottom) - all delay repeats are passed through a low-pass filter, which reduces high-frequency content
- **BP** (middle) - adds a bandpass filter to the delay signal

Below the button is the inscription FLAM, to the left of which there is a round red glow indicator connected to the inscription FLAM by a white line. The FLAM effect creates a stereophonic delay with offset left and right channels.

To the right is a round stepped regulator knob signed DRY under the minimum value and WET under the maximum value. Adjusts the balance between the "dry" signal and delay repeats. The regulator has 11 positions from 0 to 10.

To the right of it is a vertical gray button. Above it is a round red glow indicator with the inscription ON, and below it is the same indicator with the inscription GLOBAL. The delay effect can be set to global mode (Shift+On), which applies current settings to all piano and sample layers.

## AMP Block

In the bottom row of the EFFECTS section, three more blocks are placed horizontally.

The left one of these blocks is called AMP. Here are amplification effects, the block includes a complex simulation of the amplifier body and acoustic system, as well as universal tube distortions.

In its left part, three round red glow indicators are placed vertically. To the right of them are inscriptions:
- **SMALL** - simulation of a built-in tube amplifier and electric piano speakers on a reed base
- **JC** - simulation of a classic solid-state amplifier and acoustic system body
- **TWIN** - simulation of a classic tube amplifier and body with two speakers

Below is a black button, and below it is another round indicator and the inscription ALT TONE.

ALT TONE (Shift + selection button) is a timbre setting, it changes the frequency response and character of each amplifier model.

To the right at the top is a round red glow indicator with the inscription ON. Below it is a round DRIVE regulator knob. The DRIVE knob adjusts the degree of overdrive. The ON LED shows when overdrive is active. The regulator has 11 positions from 0 to 10.

To the right in this block is a vertical gray button to activate the effect. Above it is a round red glow indicator with the inscription ON.

## COMP Block

The next block in the bottom row of EFFECTS section blocks is called COMP. It is responsible for the compression effect (Compressor), which reduces the dynamic range, making low-level signals louder and high-level signals quieter.

In its left part at the top is a round red glow indicator with the inscription ACTIVE. It shows when the compression threshold is exceeded and compression is applied to the original sound.

Below it is a round COMP regulator knob. The regulator has 11 positions from 0 to 10.

To the right of it is a vertical gray button. Above it is a round red glow indicator with the inscription ON, and below it is a round indicator with the inscription GLOBAL. The compression effect can be set to global mode (Shift + ON), which applies the same settings to all piano and sample layers.

## GLOBAL REVERB Block

The last, right block in the bottom row of EFFECTS section blocks is called GLOBAL REVERB.

The global reverberation block simulates natural sound reflections in various acoustic environments, its settings are common to all active layers unless Layer Send is activated.

On the left in it is a group of indicators and a selection button. The indicators look like pairs of red glow triangles pointing left and right. There are three such pairs, they are located one below the other.

Reverb types:
- **ROOM** (top left) - reverb is reproduced as in a medium-sized room with a relatively short decay time
- **BOOTH** (middle left) - simulation of sound in a small room with a very short decay time
- **SPRING** (bottom left) - reproduces the sound of a traditional spring reverberator
- **STAGE** (top right) - stage reverb decay time is medium, natural sound atmosphere
- **HALL** (middle right) - resembles sound in a spacious hall with long decay
- **CATH** (bottom right) - Cathedral reverberator is very powerful and immersive, with very long decay time

Below under the selection button is a round red glow indicator with the inscription PRE-DELAY. When pre-delay is turned on (Shift+ reverb type selector), the wet signal is delayed relative to the dry component.

To the right are vertically two round red glow indicators and a button. To the right of the indicators are inscriptions:
- **BRIGHT** - low frequencies are slightly muted, and higher frequency content is preserved in the reverb signal
- **DARK** - high frequencies are muted

Below the button is another round indicator and the inscription CHORALE. This setting (Shift + Bright/Dark button) increases the pitch modulation of the reverb signal for a more lively and spectacular sound.

To the right is a round stepped regulator knob signed DRY under the minimum value and WET under the maximum value. The regulator has 11 positions from 0 to 10.

From the bottom in the middle from the regulator extends a white line down, and then at a right angle to the right to a round red glow indicator with the inscription LAYER SEND. This indicator is located under the button described below.

To the right of it is a vertical gray button to activate the effect. Above it is a round red glow indicator with the inscription ON, and below it is the same indicator with the inscription LAYER SEND. LAYER SEND (Shift + ON button) allows you to set an individual amount of dry/wet sound for each layer. When the LAYER SEND function is turned off, the amount of dry/wet layer for the currently focused layer is applied to all layers.

At this point, the buttons and other controls and indication on the front panel end.
