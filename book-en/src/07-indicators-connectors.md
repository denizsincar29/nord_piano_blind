# Indicators and Connectors

## Additional Indicators Above Keyboard

Additional round bulb indicators are located above the instrument keyboard. These are LEDs indicating which split position is active as part of the **KB SPLIT** keyboard split function.

**Number:** 9 indicators

**Location:** Approximately above the following notes (or slightly to the right):

1. Above **E** of the great octave
2. Above **B** of the great octave
3. Above **E** of the small octave
4. Above **B** of the small octave
5. Above **E** of the first octave
6. Above **B** of the first octave
7. Above **E** of the second octave
8. Above **B** of the second octave
9. Above **E** of the third octave

## Rear Panel Connectors

**List of connectors from left to right** (when looking at the rear panel):

### 1. Rectangular gray button **POWER ON/OFF**

### 2. Connector **AC IN**

### 3. Extensive empty space with warning text

### 4. Connector **FOOT SWITCH**

### 5. Connector **USB**

**Type:** USB Type B (square shape with cut upper corners)

### 6. Connector **MIDI OUT**

### 7. Connector **MIDI IN**

### 8. Empty space

### 9. Round connector **TRIPLE PEDAL**

### 10-14. Five identical round connectors

**Description:** With round metal convex edging

**List:**
- **SUSTAIN PEDAL**
- **VOL/CTRL PEDAL**
- **RIGHT OUT**
- **LEFT OUT**
- **HEADPHONES**

### 15. Connector **MONITOR IN**

**Description:** Similar to previous ones, but smaller in diameter by approximately two times
