# Indicators and Connectors

## Additional Indicators Above the Keyboard

There are additional round bulb indicators above the instrument keyboard. These are LEDs indicating which split position is active as part of the KB SPLIT keyboard split function.

There are nine of them, they are not signed in any way. Located approximately like this or slightly to the right:
1. Above E of the great octave
2. Above B of the great octave
3. Above E of the small octave
4. Above B of the small octave
5. Above E of the first octave
6. Above B of the first octave
7. Above E of the second octave
8. Above B of the second octave
9. Above E of the third octave

## Rear Panel Connectors

List of connectors from left to right, when looking at the rear panel:

1. Rectangular gray **POWER ON/OFF** button
2. **AC IN** connector
3. Extensive empty space with warning text
4. **FOOT SWITCH** connector
5. **USB** connector. Has a non-standard USB shape, but square with cut upper corners, this is USB type B
6. **MIDI OUT** connector
7. **MIDI IN** connector
8. Empty space
9. Round **TRIPLE PEDAL** connector
10. Next are five round connectors of the same shape with a round metal convex edging. Their list:
    - **SUSTAIN PEDAL**
    - **VOL/CTRL PEDAL**
    - **RIGHT OUT**
    - **LEFT OUT**
    - **HEADPHONES**
11. Next to them, but smaller in diameter by about two times, is the **MONITOR IN** connector
